Process

Most engagements run three to six months

The shape stays the same across three phases. What changes is where the time goes.

01

Strategy

Customer research, market research. No recommendations before I understand what's actually happening.

02

Build

I execute myself, or bring in a small team. Someone senior stays close to the work throughout.

03

Handoff

I help define whether a permanent hire is needed, and what that role should look like.

Most fractional engagements fail because the person shows up with a generic playbook and stays uninvolved. I do the research myself. I build the product myself. I stay accountable for outcomes, not just recommendations.
Where I come in

Four situations founders bring me into

Pre-seed
I turn your idea into a real product

Most ideas fail from vague scope, not bad execution. I run the customer and market research first, then tell you honestly if the idea needs to change before you spend on building it.

Proof: Took a one-line brief to a proprietary matching algorithm and 500,000+ downloads. 20+ founders, 6 countries, at Graph++.

Series A and B
I find where your growth is stuck, and fix it

Growth rarely stalls for one reason. I dig into the actual funnel, pricing, or product gap causing it, then either fix it myself or build the small team that does.

Proof: Took a regulated platform from 3.5M to 7M users. Launched a product that added $2M in revenue within 30 days. Grew trading volume 120%.

Operations
I turn your manual work into AI systems

I redesign the process before I automate it, so the AI system replaces the right work, not just the visible symptom.

Proof: Built a 6-agent research system that replaced 15+ hours of manual synthesis, and changed a founder's product direction.

AI product strategy
I turn "we need AI" into a working prototype

I decide what the AI should actually do for the user, where it fits in the product, and whether it changes how you make money — then build a real prototype, not a slide deck.

Proof: Shipped 5+ AI prototypes across health, travel, education, and research funding — each built and validated with real users.
