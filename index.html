How I can help

Product strategy and AI strategy

Product Strategy

Pre-seed through Series B

Finding what to build next, and building a plan the team can actually follow.

  • Product-market fit & customer research: talking to real customers to see if the idea holds up before you spend on building it
  • Roadmap & prioritisation: turning a long list of ideas into a sequence the team can execute
  • Growth & retention: finding where users drop off and fixing the journey itself, not just the messaging around it
  • New revenue streams: pricing and monetisation models built for the business you actually have

AI Strategy

Pre-seed through Series B

Making AI useful inside the actual product and the actual workflow.

  • Workflow optimisation: rebuilding how a team works with AI day to day (one team went from 15 days to under 2 for PRD writing)
  • Rapid prototyping: taking a spec to a deployed, working build myself, so you can test with real users instead of a slide
  • AI-driven products: deciding where AI genuinely improves the product experience, and where it's better left out

How It Works

How an engagement runs

The shape stays the same whether the work is finding a first product or fixing a growth problem. What changes is where I spend the time.

01

Strategy

I get into the actual problem first. Customer research, market research, a clear look at what's working and what isn't. No recommendations before I understand what's really going on.

02

Build

I either execute the plan myself or bring in a small team to do it, depending on what the work needs. Either way, someone senior stays close to the work the whole time.

03

Handoff

Before I leave, I help define what the permanent role should look like, if there needs to be one at all. You get through the gap without carrying a full time hire before you know if you need one, and without getting stuck if the engagement doesn't work out.

If you've been burned by fractional hires before

A lot of fractional engagements fail because the person shows up with a generic playbook and no real involvement in the work. I don't hand you a deck and disappear. I do the research myself, I build the product myself when that's what's needed, and I stay accountable for outcomes, not just recommendations.

AI learning platform
EdTech, AI-native
EU · 2026
Ask
Validate the target market and core product hypothesis for an AI-native learning platform.
Approach
Ran structured customer research and market validation, using a 6-agent AI system built to run interview synthesis with a built-in check against unsupported claims.
