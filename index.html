How It Works

How this usually works

01

A two-week diagnosis

I go through the product, the data, and your customers, and come back with what's actually going on and what I'd do about it. Some founders take that and run it themselves. That's a fine outcome.

02

Ongoing product leadership

Two to three days a week, no fixed end. I work inside the team, not next to it. For when there isn't one question, there are twenty, and you need someone senior making the calls rather than advising on them.

03

One question, answered properly

Four to six weeks, scoped to a single thing you need to know. It usually ends with a working prototype in front of real users, because that's the best way to settle the question.

If you've been burned by a fractional hire before

Most people have. The usual version is someone senior who shows up for a workshop, leaves a deck, and is unreachable by week four.

I work inside the team, in your tools, on your decisions. I write the specs and I build the prototypes.

And when the work is done, it's done. I'd rather end an engagement cleanly than stretch it out.

Case studies

Seven case studies across five sectors

The short version of each: what was asked, what I did, and what happened.

ZebPay
Fintech scale-up
India · 2021–2026
Question
How do you grow a regulated crypto platform without breaking trust or compliance?
Answer
Rebuilt onboarding and the core trading journey. Found that people knew how to buy but not what to buy, and replaced the menu with a guided decision. Found that KYC — ten minutes long and invisible to everyone — was the platform's biggest drop-off point, and cut it to under a minute. Launched three new revenue lines, and rebuilt how the product org worked with AI, including a portfolio-health dashboard personalised to each user's risk profile and interview-synthesis agents that cut research time by 20+ hours a round.
Result
User base grew 3.5M → ~7M; the guided decision flow added ~$2.1M in AUM straight after launch; QuickTrade hit $18M in peak daily volume within 30 days; PRD writing went from 15 days to under 2.

AI learning platform
EdTech, AI-native
EU · 2026
Question
Is this the right market and the right product hypothesis for an AI-native learning platform?
Answer
Ran structured customer research and market validation, using a 6-agent AI system built to run interview synthesis with a built-in check against unsupported claims.
Result
Bad news, but better to hear it now: the target age group and the founder's core hypothesis were both wrong. Redirected the strategy before further engineering spend, avoiding a costly build in the wrong direction.
